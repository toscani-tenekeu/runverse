# Code of Conduct

We want Runverse to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Our standards

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and take feedback gracefully. Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or sexual content.
- No publishing other people's private information.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other Runverse space, and when you represent the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **team.runverse@kmerhosting.com**. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove content, or warn or ban anyone who breaks these rules.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
